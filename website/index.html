Your unused tokens can fix open source.

Don-a-Token is a desktop app that donates spare ChatGPT plan capacity to open-source projects you choose — small, sandboxed Codex jobs that land as reviewable pull requests. Think Folding@home, for tokens.

Mac, Windows, and Linux. Free and open source (Apache-2.0).

How it works

  1. Sign in with ChatGPT. The app registers a user-defined agent with ChatGPT plan usage. Only eligible inference requests touch your plan — never your conversations.
  2. Set your rules. Cap donations (say, at most 10% of remaining usage), keep a reserve floor, set quiet hours, pause, or skip a week — from the app or the tray menu.
  3. Pick your projects. Choose from a manually approved registry. Jobs run unattended in isolated sandboxes (E2B Embed) and contribute back as PRs attributed to you.

Built for trust

  • Isolation first: unattended model runs execute in Firecracker sandboxes via E2B Embed, never on your bare desktop.
  • Attribution always: every contribution carries your linked identity plus the harness client id as provenance.
  • Curated registry: projects are manually approved and reviewed with maintainers before they accept donations.
  • You stay in control: usage-limit errors halt donations immediately; pause and skip are one click away.
